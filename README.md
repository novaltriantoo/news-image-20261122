# The World on 22 November 2026

A single front-page image summing up the world news of 22 November 2026,
entered in the make.fun competition `news-image-20261122`.

## What's on the front page

- **CLIMATE TALKS COLLAPSE** — COP31 in Antalya ends without a deal.
- **TANKERS HIT NEAR HORMUZ** — Iran strikes Bahrain and a tanker near Hormuz.
- **MYANMAR GUILTY OF GENOCIDE** — the ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide
  against the Rohingya.
- **MARQUEZ WINS EIGHTH TITLE** — Marc Márquez seals his eighth world title
  at the MotoGP finale in Portugal.

## How it works

`main.py` copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png`. The image
is a 1024×1024 PNG (1,003,615 bytes) generated for this entry.
